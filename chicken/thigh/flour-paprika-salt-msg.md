## Ingredients

Dredge, per 700 g thigh (quantities are a starting point, calibrate):

- Wheat flour: 100 g
- Cornstarch or rice flour: 50 g
- Hungarian paprika (hand-picked, sweet): 8 g
- Salt: 8 g
- MSG: 2 g
- Neutral oil (rapeseed/sunflower) for frying, ~1 cm in the pan

## Method

1. Flatten thighs to an even ~2 cm. Pat dry.
2. Mix the dredge. Press the chicken in firmly, shake off excess, rest 5–10 min so the coating sets.
3. Heat oil to 170–175 °C in a heavy pan.
4. Fry 4–5 min per side, in batches, to 80–85 °C core.
5. Drain on a wire rack, not paper.

## Notes

- Skillet only. In the air fryer, skip the dredge.
- Oil, not butter: milk solids burn ~150 °C. For butter flavour, add 15–20 g for the last minute.

## Variations

- Smoked: blend paprika 50/50 sweet Hungarian and pimentón.
- Caraway: add crushed caraway seed for a rye-bread note.
- Cumin–coriander: swap paprika for 1 tbsp each toasted, coarsely crushed cumin and coriander seed + 1 tsp cracked black pepper (toast whole 1–2 min, crush coarse, not powder).
- Idea, untried: fennel seed + orange zest + black pepper (may clash with lime in the sauce).
