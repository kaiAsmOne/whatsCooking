## Ingredients

Dredge, per 700 g thigh (quantities are a starting point, calibrate):

- Wheat flour: 100 g
- Rice flour or cornstarch: 50 g
- Five-spice powder: 5 g
- Salt: 8 g
- Black pepper, coarsely cracked: 2 g (optional)
- Neutral oil for frying, ~1 cm in the pan

Optional brine: buttermilk or yoghurt, 30 min to overnight in the fridge before dredging.

## Method

1. Flatten thighs to an even ~2 cm. If brined, let excess drip off; otherwise pat dry.
2. Dredge, press firmly, shake off, rest 5–10 min.
3. Fry at 170–175 °C, 4–5 min per side, to 80–85 °C core.
4. Drain on a wire rack.

## Notes

- Favourite of the dredges: "pleasant surprise".
- 30–40 % rice flour/starch gives a shatterier crust.
- Glazing with nước mắm: dust lightly with cornstarch after the dredge; holds up better under a glaze toss.
- Savoury lean: drop any paprika, go five-spice + coarse black pepper only.
