## Ingredients

Marinade, per 700 g thigh:

- Fresh ginger, finely grated: 15 g
- Fresh garlic, finely grated: 15 g (3 cloves)
- Greek yoghurt: 100 g
- Salt: 6–7 g
- White pepper: 2.5 g
- MSG: 2 g
- Oil spray for cooking

## Method

1. Mix the marinade, coat the chicken. Fridge, 60–120 min (up to 4 h is fine, not past 8 h).
2. Preheat air fryer to 200 °C.
3. Single layer, 12–15 min, flip halfway.
4. Pull at 80–85 °C core. Rest 3 min.
5. Serve with rice, vegetables and the nước mắm sauce on the side.

## Notes

- No dredge in the air fryer. Dredge is for the skillet.
- If the yoghurt film browns before the centre is done: 190 °C, +2–3 min.
- Too dry: shorten by 2 min, and/or add 1 tbsp oil to the marinade.
