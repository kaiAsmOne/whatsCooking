## Ingredients

- Pork belly, skin on: 540 g
- Garlic, grated: 10 g
- Five-spice: 2 g
- White pepper: 2 g
- Fish sauce: 10 ml
- Salt: only if the belly is unsalted (then ~6 g on the meat side)

## Method

1. Day before: simmer the whole piece 15 min. Dry completely.
2. Prick the skin densely with a skewer. Score the meat side.
3. Rub the meat side only with garlic, five-spice, white pepper, fish sauce. Skin stays clean and dry.
4. Fridge, uncovered, skin up, overnight.
5. Air fryer, skin up: 160 °C 30 min, then 210 °C 15–20 min until the skin puffs and blisters.
6. Rest 10 min, cut into strips.

## Notes

- The overnight dry is what makes the crackling. Saved for weekends.
