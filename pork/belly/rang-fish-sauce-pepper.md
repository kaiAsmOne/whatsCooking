## Ingredients

- Lightly salted pork belly, skin on, sliced 5 mm: 540 g
- Shallots, thinly sliced: 60 g
- Garlic, minced: 10 g
- Fish sauce (sild): 10–15 ml, start at 10 ml, the meat is already salted
- Water: 30 ml
- Sugar: none (v2). Add 1–2 g only if it tastes flat
- Black pepper, coarsely cracked: 3 g
- Habanero (liquid): to taste
- Spring onion, 3 cm lengths: 20 g

## Method

1. Firm the belly 20–30 min in the freezer, then slice 5 mm.
2. Fry one test slice and taste. If clearly salty, blanch all slices 2 min, drain, dry well.
3. Render in **two batches** (pan takes ~350–400 g in one layer): cold dry pan, medium heat, 8–10 min per batch, turning, until edges are golden. Move batch 1 to a plate.
4. Pour off fat, leave ~1 tbsp. Return all the meat to the pan.
5. Shallot and garlic, 1–2 min, until the shallot takes colour. This browning replaces the sugar.
6. Fish sauce + water, high heat, toss 2–3 min until reduced to a glaze that clings.
7. Off heat: pepper, habanero, spring onion.

## Notes

- Without sugar the colour comes from rendering, browned shallot and reduced fish sauce. Let step 5 go a bit darker than feels comfortable.
- Shallots are ~7–8 % sugar: 60 g browned shallot ≈ 4–5 g caramelised sugar, concentrated in the glaze. That, not the 5 g added sugar alone, is why v1 tasted sweet. Fewer shallots = less sweet.
- The belly itself is negligible: label says ~0.1 % sugar (≈ 0.5 g per 540 g).
- If it still reads sweet: a squeeze of lime at the end, not more fish sauce.
- Keep the rendered fat in a jar for frying.
- Serve with rice and something light and sour. Kålstuing was too heavy with this.
- Weekend version with crackling: [crispy-skin-air-fryer](crispy-skin-air-fryer.md).
