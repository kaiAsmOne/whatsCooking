## Ingredients

- Tynnribbe, rind on: 3 kg (500 g per person)
- Salt: generous rub. Original gives no amount; starting point ~10 g per kg
- Black pepper, coarsely ground: generous. Starting point ~3 g per kg
- Chicken stock: 2.5 dl
- Water: 7.5 dl
- Bay leaves: 3 (≈ 0.6 g)
- Star anise: 2 (≈ 1 g)
- Cinnamon stick: ½ (≈ 2 g)
- Cloves: 6 (≈ 0.5 g)
- Garlic, whole cloves: 30 g (6 cloves)
- Fresh ginger, in pieces: 20 g (thumb-sized)

## Method

Timeline for dinner at 17:30: salt the evening before, oven on around 08:00.

1. **Evening before:** rub salt and pepper into all the cuts and crevices, especially the rind. Wrap in foil, fridge overnight.
2. **Steam, 45 min:** oven 180 °C, top and bottom heat, no fan. Ribbe rind side **down** in a deep dish. Spices, garlic and ginger around it, pour over stock and water. Seal with foil, 45 min.
3. **Flip:** take it out, turn the oven down to 90 °C (still no fan). Shape the foil into a rounded "football" and lay it under the ribbe, so it sits rind side **up** and arched. Probe in the thickest part, clear of bone. Back in once the oven is at 90 °C.
4. **Slow-roast, ~7 h:** until 80 °C core.
5. **Crackling, ~20 min:** oven to 275 °C until the rind puffs. Grill element works too, but stay at the oven door.
6. **Rest 45 min, uncovered.** Covering ruins the crackling. It is still hot when you carve.

## Notes

- Author: Espen Nersveen, Heia Mat. Method paraphrased; quantities as published.
- The arched foil "bridge" makes the fat run off the rind and the rind dry out, which is what lets it pop at 275 °C.
- Ribbe from the shop is usually pre-scored (risset). If not, score the rind in 1 cm strips before salting.
- From the comments: higher oven temperature shortens the time, but then the probe decides. After reaching 80 °C it can be held at 75–80 °C until serving.
- Two ribber: cook together on fan, pop the rind one at a time.
- Familieribbe is leaner and dries out more easily. Use proper tynnribbe.
- The braising liquid (stock, star anise, ginger) is a good base for the gravy.
- Smaller piece: the 540 g belly method in [crispy-skin-air-fryer](../belly/crispy-skin-air-fryer.md) is the weeknight cousin.
