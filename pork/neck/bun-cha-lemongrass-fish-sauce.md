## Ingredients

Marinade, per 1 kg neck:

- Lemongrass, tender core, very finely minced or microplaned: 20 g (≈ 2 stalks)
- Shallot, finely grated: 40 g
- Garlic, finely grated: 20 g
- Fish sauce (sild): 30 ml
- Black pepper, coarsely cracked: 4 g
- MSG: 2 g
- Neutral oil: 15 ml
- Sugar: none. If more lacquer wanted, try 5 g next time

Dipping broth with pickled kohlrabi:

- Kohlrabi, peeled, 2 mm slices, 2–3 cm across: 200 g
- Carrot, same cut: 50 g (optional)
- Salt for the vegetables: 3 g
- Fish sauce: 45 ml
- Warm water: 250 ml
- Lime juice or rice vinegar: 40 ml
- Garlic, thinly sliced: 10 g
- Habanero (liquid): to taste
- Black pepper: a pinch

To serve:

- Rice vermicelli (bún): 320 g dry (80 g per person)
- Lettuce, mint, coriander
- Pickled spisskål, drained (optional extra)

## Method

1. **Lemongrass:** use the bottom 8–10 cm, peel off the tough outer layers down to the pale core, mince very fine. Coarse bits stay woody.
2. **Marinate:** rub the marinade into the chops. At least 1 h, overnight in the fridge is fine (no yoghurt, so no mushiness).
3. **Kohlrabi:** toss the slices with 3 g salt, 15 min. Rinse and squeeze dry.
4. **Broth:** mix fish sauce, warm water, lime, garlic, habanero, pepper. Add the kohlrabi and carrot 30–60 min before eating.
5. **Noodles and herbs:** cook or soak the vermicelli per pack, rinse cold, drain. Wash the herbs.
6. **Grill:** air fryer preheated to 200 °C, single layer, 10–14 min, flip halfway. Two batches for 1 kg. Pull at 68–70 °C core.
7. **Serve:** rest 3 min, cut into 1 cm strips, put the strips in the broth. Everyone dips noodles and herbs into it.

## Notes

- Alternatives to the air fryer: cast-iron grill pan on high, or oven grill at the top setting.
- First batch can wait in the broth while the second cooks.
- Neck is fatty enough to stay juicy at 68–70 °C.
- Classic Hanoi bún chả also has grilled pork patties and a sweeter broth; this version is the low-sugar Northern register.
- Fallback with the same cut: the chicken thigh ginger–garlic–white pepper yoghurt marinade, ×1.4 for 1 kg, same air fryer method.
