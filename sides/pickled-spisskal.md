## Ingredients

- Spisskål, finely shredded: 100 g (≈ 3 dl)
- Apple cider vinegar: 1.2 dl
- Water: 2 dl
- Sugar: 85 g (1 dl)
- Salt (herb salt works well): 3 g

## Method

1. Bring water, vinegar, sugar and salt to the boil, stirring until dissolved.
2. Put the shredded cabbage in a bowl and pour the hot brine over it.
3. Cool, then chill until serving.
4. Drain well before serving. This is what keeps the sweetness in check.

## Notes

- Shared recipe, Norwegian original: "3 dl finsnittet spisskål, 0,5–1 dl eplecidereddik, 1 dl sukker, 1,5 dl vann, litt salt (gjerne urtesalt)".
- Brine volume is set by the container: the cabbage must be fully covered. To scale the brine, keep vinegar : water : sugar at about 1.2 : 2 : 1 dl.
- Most of the sugar stays in the brine. Lift the cabbage out with a fork and the sweetness on the plate is far lower than 85 g suggests.
- Leftovers keep in a large glass jar in the fridge.
- Good next to fatty pork: the rang, or the crispy-skin belly.
