## Ingredients

- White cabbage (or spisskål), wedges or 3 cm ribbons: 400 g
- Lime juice: 20 ml
- Fish sauce: 10 ml
- Water: 10 ml
- Garlic, grated: 5 g
- Black pepper: a pinch
- Habanero (liquid): optional

## Method

1. Steam the cabbage 5–7 min (ribbons) or 8–10 min (wedges), until just tender and still bright.
2. Mix lime, fish sauce, water, garlic, pepper.
3. Spoon the dressing over the hot cabbage, or serve it on the side for dipping.

## Notes

- Based on Northern bắp cải luộc chấm nước mắm. The traditional version mashes a soft-boiled egg into the dip, which works well if you want more on the plate.
- Spisskål cooks faster: check at 4 min.
- With fatty pork: lean on the lime.
