## Ingredients

- Fish sauce (sild): 45 ml
- Water: 30 ml
- Sugar: 15 g (reduce toward 10 g for your palate)
- Lime juice, fresh: 30 ml
- Fresh garlic, minced: 10 g (2 cloves)
- Habanero, liquid: to taste
- Black pepper, cracked: generous pinch

## Method

1. Warm the water slightly, dissolve the sugar.
2. Stir in fish sauce and lime juice.
3. Add garlic, chili and pepper last.
4. Taste: savoury first, then sour, sweetness barely there.

## Uses

- Dip, as is. Keeps the crust crisp.
- Glaze: reduce 2–3 min until syrupy, toss just-fried chicken 30 s off the heat.
